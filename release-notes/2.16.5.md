## What's new in 2.16.5

- **Fixed: iPhones refused Apple Wallet passes.** Tapping *Add to Apple Wallet* gave "Safari can't download this file" (or "This pass can't be installed"), for one ticket and for several. The date and time on the pass caused it; they are now shown as text in the date and time format of the *Configuration*, like on the PDF ticket. Customers who couldn't add their tickets before can use the same button in their ticket mail again.
