## What's new in 2.26.0

### New
- **A new way of checking out.** As part of our ongoing UX/UI optimization, customers can now order on a single page. In *Configuration* > *Checkout* the new setting *Checkout layout* lets you choose: *Separate steps* (the default) is the route you know, with a basket, a details page and a payment page. *Combined page* brings the basket, the details form and the payment together on one page called *Checkout*. The order sits beside the form (above it on a phone) and stays in view while the customer fills in the details, and the pay button shows the amount.
- **Change the order right on the checkout page.** Customers add or remove tickets, enter a discount code and add a note without leaving the page. Totals, service fee, discount and the amount on the pay button follow at once, and what the customer already typed stays in place.
- **One way to the checkout.** With the combined page every link to the basket, from the basket module, the ticket pages and the seat chart, leads to the checkout page. The step bar above the pages has two steps: *Choose tickets* and *Checkout*.

### Improvements
- **Waiting-list tickets in the basket are one line per ticket, with a quantity.** Like other tickets they now have a plus and a minus button and a remove button for the whole line, in the basket and on the combined checkout page, instead of one row per signup. The maximum per order of a ticket now also counts the waiting list.
- **No more "Verifying... please wait" popup with the Altcha captcha** on the details page and on the combined page. The captcha is checked as soon as the page opens, and the button waits for it when the customer is quicker than that. A form that is not complete is reported first, before the captcha starts.

### Check before you update
- **Nothing changes until you switch it on.** The setting starts on *Separate steps*. Try the combined page on a test order first, including a free order, a discount code and, if you use it, the waiting list.
- **Overrides of the cart and payment pages.** The order table of the cart and payment pages now comes from shared layouts (`cart_lines`, `order_summary`, `payment_methods`, `payment_terms`, `checkout_fields`, `checkout_notices`, `checkout_pay`). Overrides of the pages themselves keep working; if you want the new behaviour in an override, override the layout. An override of the step bar (`steps`) shows the classic four steps also with the combined page.
