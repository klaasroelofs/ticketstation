## What's new in 2.6.14

### Tickets and seat plans
- **From a ticket straight to its seat plan.** A main ticket with a seat plan now has a *Save & Seat Plan* button on its edit screen. It saves the ticket first, so no changes are lost, and then opens the seat plan editor.
- **And back again.** The seat plan editor has an *Edit Ticket* button that takes you to the ticket's edit screen. If the seat plan has unsaved changes, your browser asks first, just like when you close the editor.
