## What's new in 2.16.4

- **Order emails sent from the Box Office now use the site language.** When you resend tickets from the backend, the seat label (for example "Stoelnummer") in the order list and the old `{paymentstatus}` placeholder ("Paid" / "Unpaid") were translated in your own backend language. They now always follow the language of the website, like the tickets themselves.
