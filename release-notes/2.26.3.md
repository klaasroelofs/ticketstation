## What's new in 2.26.3

### Fixed
- **"Prevent single empty seats" now also works on the combined checkout page.** Removing a seat there could leave a single empty seat without any warning. The customer is now sent back to the seat plan, with the single seats marked, just like on the basket page.
- **The exception to "Prevent single empty seats" is fairer.** A customer could leave a single empty seat by picking one seat of a free pair, even when the rest of the order could just as well fill a longer row. That is now refused. When it truly cannot be avoided, for example when you want 31 of the 32 free seats in a block, the choice is still allowed. A free seat directly across from the leftover seat, as at a long table, is taken into account.
- **The remove button on the seat plan showed a wrong text in Dutch.** It read "Plaats %s verwijderen 102 uit bestelling" instead of "Verwijder stoel 102 uit bestelling". The text is correct again.
- **The Added column of the events list counts the seats of seated tickets.** The total for an event now uses the number of seats on the chart for seated tickets.
