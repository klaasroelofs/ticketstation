## What's new in 2.9.1

### Refunds through Mollie
- **Refund an order from the Box Office.** In a paid order, *Payment* › *Refund...* opens a refund screen. Refund the whole order or part of it: the amount follows the tickets you choose, with the service fee if you tick it, or type an amount of your own. For an order paid through Mollie the refund is made at Mollie straight away; for an order paid in another way (cash, bank transfer) the refund is registered and you pay the money back yourself.
- **Choose what happens to each ticket.** *Refunded, stays valid* (for example a gesture of goodwill), *Invalid, place stays taken* (refused at the door, not sold again) or *Invalid and released* (refused at the door, and the ticket or seat is for sale again, with the waiting list first when it is on).
- **Nothing changes while a refund can still be cancelled.** The tickets only change once Mollie has processed the refund, usually the next working day. Until then the order shows *Waiting for Mollie* at those tickets; if you cancel the refund in the Mollie Dashboard, the tickets stay as they were.
- **Refunds made in the Mollie Dashboard are picked up.** A refund or chargeback that you or a colleague made in Mollie appears under *Needs attention* on the Dashboard. Click it to decide what happens to the tickets; the tickets whose prices make up the refunded amount are already proposed. Once you have decided, the notice disappears.
- **The order always stays in the Box Office.** The list and the order show *Partly refunded* or *Refunded*, the order has a *Refunds* block with every refund and its status, and each ticket shows what happened to it. The payment status filter has a new option, *Refund needs attention*.
- **A refund that fails after the tickets were dealt with**, or a chargeback that is reversed, is reported under *Needs attention*.
- **Sync with Mollie** in an order fetches its refunds from Mollie, for example refunds made before this version.
- Invalid tickets are refused by the scanner and left out of the ticket files, so *Resend*, *Download* and the *Lost tickets* page only hold valid tickets.
- The Documentation has a new *Refunds* section in the Box Office topic.

## Check before you update
- **Mark as refunded is gone.** The Box Office no longer has the *Mark as refunded* action; use *Refund...* in the order instead. Orders you marked as refunded earlier keep that status, and the *Refunded* filter still shows them.
- Refunding needs the *Register payments* permission, like the other payment actions.
