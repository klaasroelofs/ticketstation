## What's new in 2.23.0

### Live updates for Apple Wallet
- **Passes follow the ticket.** With *Live updates* switched on (Configuration, Wallet tab, under Apple Wallet), the passes customers saved in Apple Wallet are kept in line with their tickets: a refunded or blocked ticket makes its pass void, a new QR code replaces the old one, and a changed event is brought in line when you say so. An iPhone is told about a change by a push notification and then fetches the new pass by itself.
- **Messages on the passes.** A message you send shows on the pass as a notification, the same as for Google Wallet.
- **What you need.** The site has to be on https. Ticketstation installs a new plugin, *System - Ticketstation*, that answers the requests of Apple Wallet; keep it enabled. For the notifications your server's cURL has to support HTTP/2. Without it a change reaches the phone when the customer opens Wallet. Below the setting the Configuration shows what your server supports and the web service address the passes get.
- **Only new passes can be updated.** A pass that was added before you switched live updates on can't be updated, as before. Customers with an older pass add their tickets once more. A new QR code now replaces the pass in the wallet instead of making a second one.

### The new Communication screen
- **One place for talking to the buyers of an event.** *Mail the buyers* in the Events list is now *Communication*. It has two actions that are clearly apart: a **message to the buyers** (subject and text, with suggestions for a new time, another location or a cancellation, a test mail to yourself, and a choice to send it by mail, to the passes in the wallets, or both) and **update the tickets in the wallets** (no text needed: it brings the passes in line with the event as it is now, and shows how many Google and Apple passes it reaches).
- **What has been sent.** A third part shows how many orders got a message and a reminder, when, and what is still waiting for the wallets or went wrong.
- **A notice after you change something.** When you save an event or ticket with a changed date, time, venue or name, and the event has buyers, a notice points to the Communication screen.

### Deleted orders and tickets leave the wallets
- **A removed ticket doesn't stay as a valid pass.** When you delete an order or tickets from an order in the Box Office, a Google pass expires and an Apple pass is marked void and expired. Neither wallet allows a pass to be taken out of the customer's wallet from outside, so the customer can still delete it themselves. For Apple, the copy of the void pass that is kept for the phones has no QR code and nothing about the customer or the order.

### Documentation
- The Apple &amp; Google Wallet topic explains the live updates for both wallets, the Communication screen and the Things to know.

## Check before you update
- **Live updates for Apple are off after the update.** Nothing changes for your passes until you switch it on in the Configuration (Wallet tab).
- **A new plugin is installed and enabled.** *System - Ticketstation* does nothing while live updates for Apple are off.
