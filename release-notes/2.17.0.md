## What's new in 2.17.0

### Smaller ticket PDFs
- **Orders with several tickets now give a much smaller PDF.** The ticket design used to be stored again for every ticket in the PDF, so 30 tickets with a 1 MB design came to over 30 MB. The design is now stored once per order. A test order of 4 tickets went from 1.2 MB to 0.3 MB.
- **A JPG design is scaled down when you upload it.** A picture that is larger than the ticket needs (more than 200 dpi at the ticket size) is reduced and saved again with a moderate quality, and you get a message with the new size. Small designs are left alone. A PDF design is never changed, so keep that one small yourself.

### New file names
- **The ticket PDFs have new names.** An order with one ticket is now `Ticket-<order code>.pdf`, an order with several tickets `Tickets-<order code>.pdf`. This is the name the customer sees on the attachment and on the download.

## Check before you update
- **Existing ticket files are renamed during the update.** If a file cannot be renamed, it is made again the next time it is needed, for instance when you send the tickets again.
