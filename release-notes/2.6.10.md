## What's new in 2.6.10

### Ticket page
- **Clear feedback after adding tickets.** The button is now called *Add to basket*. After a click, a message such as "2 × Adult added to your basket" stays on the page, with a *Go to basket* button next to it. It no longer fades away after a few seconds. Error messages also stay visible until the next attempt.
- The message after joining the waiting list gets the same *Go to basket* button, and now tells the customer to go to the basket to leave their details.
- **Clearer minimum and maximum.** The minimum and maximum quantity of a ticket apply to that ticket, not to the whole order. The texts now say so: "Maximum of 8 per order" under the ticket, and "You can order at most 8 of this ticket" when the limit is reached.
- **Event description and image.** The ticket page and the seat chart page now show the event's description and background image, just like the event list. Visitors who arrive through a direct link can now see what the event is about. A ticket's own background image, when it has one, is used instead of the event's.

### After payment
- **Add to calendar.** The payment result page has an *Add to calendar* button. It downloads a calendar file (.ics) with every event in the order: name, start and end time, and the venue with its address. The same file is attached to the email with the tickets, so customers can add the events to their calendar from their inbox too.
- **The download section no longer disappears.** The *Download tickets* button now stays until the tickets have actually been downloaded. Before, it was gone after a page reload even if the customer never clicked it. After the download, the page says the tickets are also in the email, and links to the *Lost tickets* page, where customers can have them emailed again.

## Check before you update
- The ticket page, the seat chart page and the payment result page have new markup. If your template overrides `event/default.php`, `seatedevent/default.php` or `paymentresult/default.php` of com_ticketstation, compare it with the new version.
- The ticket email now carries a second attachment, the calendar file. It is added automatically and needs no setting.
