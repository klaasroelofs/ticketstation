## What's new in 2.25.1

### Check before you update
- **Test mode is now closed to customers with an account.** While the payment provider is in test mode, only users with the new permission *Order in test mode* can see the tickets and order on the website. Before, any logged-in user could, which on a site where customers register meant they could place real orders with a test payment. Managers and Administrators get the permission when you update, and Super Users always have it. If someone else tests your shop, give their group the permission under *Options* > *Permissions*.

### New
- **Excel export in the Box Office.** The new *Excel* button next to *Export* downloads the guest list as an Excel file (.xlsx). Phone numbers and codes stay text, so a leading zero is kept, which a CSV file opened in a spreadsheet can lose. In a right-to-left backend the sheet reads right to left.
- **Name and email address filled in at checkout.** A logged-in customer starts with the name and email address of their account. The button *Booking for someone else?* clears them.

### Improvements
- **Dates in the exports follow your settings.** The date columns of the CSV and Excel export use the date notation of the Configuration and the calendar of the site language, such as the Persian calendar.

### Fixes
- **The ticket layout editor works in right-to-left backends.** In a right-to-left language the texts ran off the left edge of the ticket and overlapped their frames. The ticket now keeps its left-to-right layout, while Persian and Arabic text still reads correctly.
- **New tickets and events no longer start with a wrong date.** A new ticket starts at the current time and a new event on today's date. A date field that was left empty used to be saved as 1 January 1970; it now falls back to the current time.
- **Order dates in the right time zone.** The order date in the customer list, in the ticket mail and in the confirmation of the waiting list showed the stored UTC time, so late in the evening it could be the wrong day. They now use the time zone of your site.
- **No more PHP 8.4 notice on the CSV export.**
