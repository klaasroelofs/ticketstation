## What's new in 2.6.7

### Privacy
- **Deleting an order now deletes its history too.** The history held the email address the tickets and invoices were sent to and, for orders removed by the automatic cleanup, the IP address. The history that earlier deletions left behind is cleared during this update. Orders shown as *Removed* in the Box Office keep their history until you delete them there.
- The Mollie log is now kept in Joomla's log folder (`administrator/logs/com_ticketstation_mollie.php`), which can't be opened from the web. The old log file is removed during the update.

### Fixes
- **Customer details:** what a customer typed at checkout (name, address and so on) is now shown safely in the backend form, so it can no longer inject HTML or scripts. The order history on that screen now shows what the customer paid or has to pay, including discount and service fee, as in the Box Office.
- **Saving settings:** when Configuration, Mollie, a customer or a coupon could not be saved, the message now shows as an error, and *Save & Close* stays on the screen instead of reporting that it was saved.
- **Service fee text:** with a percentage service fee, the event list now says "2% of the order amount" instead of "2% per ticket", which is how the fee is actually calculated.
