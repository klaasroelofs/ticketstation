## What's new in 2.34.0

### Arabic, Persian and other scripts on tickets and invoices
- **Names and texts in Arabic and Persian are printed correctly.** Letters are joined the way the script requires and the text runs from right to left, also when it is mixed with Latin letters and numbers. Until now every such letter became a question mark on the ticket and on the invoice.
- **Cyrillic, Greek and other letters work too.** A text with letters the chosen ticket font does not have, such as Polish or Turkish letters, Cyrillic or Greek, is printed in DejaVu Sans, which is embedded in the PDF. The rest of the ticket keeps your font.
- **Nothing moves on existing tickets.** The new PDF library places text exactly where the layout editor and your saved layouts expect it; tickets with a design of their own look the same as before.

### Fixed
- **A tab in the address format no longer swallows the space** between first and last name on the invoice (it printed "FirstLast").

## Check before you update
- **Only if you have your own code that uses the FPDF library from Ticketstation's vendor folder:** it is no longer included. Tickets and invoices are made with TCPDF now (and FPDI for the ticket designs).
- **The update is about 0.6 MB larger,** because TCPDF and the DejaVu Sans font come with it.
