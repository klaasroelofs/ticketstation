## What's new in 2.35.0

### Right-to-left scripts on the ticket
- **Text alignment per ticket.** The Ticket Layout tab has a new setting, *Text alignment*. With *Left* (the default, as it always was) a text starts at the position you set. With *Right* a text ends at that position and runs to the left, which is how you place texts in Arabic and Persian. The order number, the QR code and a centred order reference are not affected. Existing tickets keep *Left*.
- **The built-in layout follows.** On a ticket without positions of its own, the default fields move to the right margin when you switch to *Right*.

### The layout editor
- **Mixed texts read the way the PDF prints them.** A text that starts in Arabic or Persian and contains Latin letters or numbers was drawn in the other order in the editor than on the ticket.
- **The frames have the right width for texts in other scripts.** Arabic, Persian, Cyrillic, Greek and Polish or Turkish letters are printed in DejaVu Sans; the editor now measures them with that font, so the frame and the "does not fit" warning are right.
- **Snap to the right edge.** While dragging, a field also lines up with the right edge of other fields.

### Documentation
- The Documentation explains the new setting and what is printed in which script, and the field's help text says what Left and Right mean.
