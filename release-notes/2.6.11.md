## What's new in 2.6.11

### Waiting list
- **No second confirmation email.** When a place became free, a customer on the waiting list received the payment link as intended. But when they followed that link and completed checkout, they were sent the waiting-list confirmation email again for the same tickets. That no longer happens: the confirmation email only goes out for waiting-list signups that have not had it yet.

### Emails
- **Tidier order list.** The `{orderlist}` field in the email templates now shows each ticket as "Event - Ticket (date) - price", for example *Christmas concert - Bus ticket (30-11-2026) - € 11,00*. The square brackets and the internal number in front of each line are gone. A sub-ticket is shown with the name of its main ticket, and a seated ticket with its seat number at the end.

## Check before you update
- Every email that uses `{orderlist}` (tickets after payment, resent tickets, payment link and waiting-list confirmation) gets the new layout, and the event name is now part of each line. If your templates also mention the event name next to `{orderlist}`, you may want to remove it there.
