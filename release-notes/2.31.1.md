## What's new in 2.31.1

### Changed
- **The switch for the automatic tasks has moved and has a clearer name.** *Automatic removal of unfinished/pending tickets* on the Orders tab is now *Run automatic tasks* on the General tab, in its own card *Automatic tasks*. The switch did more than clean up: it also publishes and unpublishes tickets and events on their dates, ends a ticket at its end date, and shows the announcements of sales that are about to start. The description now says so.
- **The removal periods stay on the Orders tab.** *Remove Unfinished Orders after* and *Remove Unpaid/Pending Orders after* are in the card *Removal of unfinished and unpaid orders*, which points to the switch on the General tab.
- **The help texts and the Documentation page use the new name.** Every place that mentioned the old name and the Orders tab, such as the help for *Use Auto Publish* and *Use Sale Stop*, now points to *Run automatic tasks* on the General tab.

## Check before you update
- **Nothing changes in how your shop behaves.** The setting keeps its value and does the same as before; only its place and name are new.
