## What's new in 2.24.0

### Check before you update
- **Prices in the backend now follow your price notation.** The Box Office, the refund screen, the clients list and the coupons list used to write amounts as `1234,50` whatever you had chosen. They now use the notation of *Configuration* > *Prices*, so a site set to, for example, `1.234,50` sees that in the backend too. Your existing notation is carried over automatically; check that the amounts on the website and in the backend still read the way you want.

### New
- **Set the price notation yourself.** Instead of choosing one of twelve fixed notations, you now set the number of decimals, the decimal point, the thousands separator and whether the currency symbol comes before or after the amount. An example under the fields shows the result as you change them. This makes notations possible that were not on the list, such as `1,235 SAR`, and amounts without decimals.
- **Whole amounts without decimals.** Choose *Only when needed* to show 12 instead of 12.00 and 12.50 as 12.50, or *no decimals* for currencies that don't use them.
- **Your own date and time format.** The date and time notations are now a field in which you can pick a suggestion or type your own PHP format, such as `Y-m-d` or `H:i:s`.

### Improvements
- **The ticket price field shows your currency.** The symbol in front of the *Price per ticket* field is the currency of the Configuration instead of always a euro sign.
- **A decimal comma works in the ticket price.** `12,50` is now saved as 12.50 instead of 12.
- **Clearer help texts** for the price, date and time notations and the ticket price, and an updated Configuration topic in the documentation.
