## What's new in 2.30.1

### Improved
- **Yes/No settings are switches.** On the Configuration screen every Yes/No setting is now a switch, like the on/off settings in Joomla's own configuration, instead of a dropdown. One click flips it, and you can see the state at a glance.
- **A clearer scanner screen.** The scanner settings and the events and tickets a scanner may scan are switches too. A child ticket is no longer listed as a separate choice: it simply shows *Included with parent* when its parent ticket is switched on, because scanning for a parent ticket always covers its child tickets. A child ticket that was assigned on its own earlier, without its parent, keeps its switch so nothing changes unnoticed.
- **Confirmation in Joomla's own dialog.** Switching the shop mode and deleting test data now ask for confirmation in Joomla's dialog instead of the browser's pop-up.
- **We keep polishing the look and feel.** This release continues the work on a clearer, more consistent interface in the administration.

### Fixed
- **Switching the shop mode after your session expired** no longer lands you on the Payments screen after you log in again; you come back to the control panel.
