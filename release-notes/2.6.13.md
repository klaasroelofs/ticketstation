## What's new in 2.6.13

### Seat chart
- **Seat details on hover or tap.** When customers hover over a seat, move to it with the keyboard or tap it on a phone, they now see the row and seat number, the price and whether the seat is taken or already chosen. A free seat that can be sold in several price categories shows all of those prices.

### Basket and payment page
- **Seats grouped by ticket.** Seated tickets used to be listed by seat number only, whatever ticket they belonged to. They are now grouped per main ticket, with the earliest date first, and a sub-ticket is listed with its main ticket. Within a main ticket, tickets without a seat come first. The seats follow per price category, the most expensive first as in the seat chart legend, each sorted by seat number.

### Tickets
- **PDF tickets grouped by main ticket.** The combined PDF now contains all tickets of one main ticket before the next, with the earliest date first; sub-tickets go with their main ticket. Within each group the tickets stay sorted by row and seat. The number printed on each ticket ("3/33") follows the same order.
- **Same order after regenerating.** When tickets were regenerated from the Box Office, the combined PDF was sorted by seat number only, so its page order could differ from the numbers printed on the tickets. It now uses the same order as the email with the tickets.
