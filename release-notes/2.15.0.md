## What's new in 2.15.0

### Service fee
- **New option: Fixed and variable.** Under *Configuration* » *Checkout & payment* the *Service Fee* can now combine a percentage with a fixed amount. The customer pays the *Variable Fee* percentage of the order total after any discount, plus the *Fixed Fee* amount on top. The cart, the payment page and the order in the Box Office show it as, for example, *Service fee (2.5% + € 0,50)*, and the event list mentions both parts.
- When a ticket is removed from such an order, the fixed part stays and the percentage is calculated again over what is left. A free order has no service fee, also not the fixed part.

### Mollie settings
- *Test Mode* now offers *On* and *Off* instead of *Yes* and *No*.
