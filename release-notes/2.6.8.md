## What's new in 2.6.8

### Ordering
- **Clearer details form.** When something is missing or wrong, the form now shows every problem at once, each next to its own field, and keeps what the customer already typed. Before, it showed one problem at a time and emptied the form. The browser also points out empty required fields before the form is sent. The country and salutation of a returning customer are filled in as well.
- **"Only a few tickets left!"** now appears on the ticket page once 10% or less is left, the same moment the availability bar turns red. Before, it practically never showed.
- **A message when something goes wrong.** If adding tickets or choosing a seat fails, for example because the session has expired, the customer now gets a message to reload the page. Before, nothing happened on the ticket page, and the seat page showed a bare error code.
- **Dates written out in full.** The ticket page, seat page, shopping basket and payment page now show the date as on the event list, in the site's language, with the start time: "Saturday 10 October 2026, 20:30".

## Check before you update
- Every field you show on the details form must be filled in, except the third address line. That already applied to most fields, but the **postcode** is now required too when *Show Zipcode* is on.
