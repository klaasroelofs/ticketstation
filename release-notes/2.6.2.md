## What's new in 2.6.2

### Documentation page
- New search field: results appear while you type. Topics without a match are hidden, matching sections open by themselves and the found words are highlighted. Press `/` to start searching, Enter to jump to the first match and Esc to clear the search.
- The topics are grouped (Basics, Events & sales, Orders, payments & email, On the day, Data & maintenance) in a sidebar that stays in view and shows the topic you are reading. On a phone it is a drop-down menu.
- Every section has its own link: hover over a heading and click the link icon to copy it.
- New buttons to expand or collapse all sections and to print the documentation with all sections expanded.
- A "Back to top" button appears when you scroll down.

### Help button
- Every Ticketstation screen in the backend now has a **Help** button in the toolbar. It opens the matching part of the documentation in a new tab.
