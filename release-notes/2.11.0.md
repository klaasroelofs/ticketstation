## What's new in 2.11.0

### Prevent single empty seats
- **A new setting per seat plan.** In the seat plan editor, tab *Display*, *Prevent single empty seats* stops customers from leaving a single free seat that is hard to sell. It is off by default, so nothing changes until you switch it on for a seat plan.
- **What is allowed.** A customer may not leave one free seat between their choice and a taken seat, or between their choice and the end of a row or an aisle. Choosing seats in the middle of a free stretch is fine as long as at least two seats stay free on either side. When a row leaves no other way (for example 3 free seats and the customer wants 2), a single seat is allowed.
- **Long tables.** At a table with seats on both sides under one row name, a single free seat is fine when the seat directly across from it is free too, as the two can still be sold together.
- **Rows are recognised by their name and position**, not by seat number, so odd/even numbering, curved and vertical rows work. A gap in a row wider than about half a seat counts as an aisle. Seats without a row name are left out.
- **Clear for the customer.** With the setting on, the *Instructions* on the seat page explain the rule with examples. When the customer clicks *Continue*, seats that would be left on their own are ringed in red with a message. The shopping basket and checkout check the choice again and send the customer back to the seat plan with the reason.
- The box office and reservations are not restricted.

### Seat numbers with leading zeros
- **Seat numbers keep their leading zeros.** Type *01* as the first number of a new row or block, or when renumbering, and the seats are numbered 01, 02 … 09, 10, 11. This keeps rows with a number readable: row 1 reads 101, 102 … instead of 11, 12 …. Typing *1* numbers the seats 1, 2, 3 as before.
- Lists in the Box Office, reservations and ticket files sort seat numbers by their value, so 9 comes before 10.

### Other changes
- **Payments use version 4 of Mollie's payment library.** Nothing changes in how you or your customers pay or refund. If a payment or refund behaves differently after the update, please report it.
