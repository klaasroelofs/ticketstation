## What's new in 2.26.2

### Improvements
- **Seats of the same ticket type are one line in the basket.** With seated events every seat used to get its own row. Seats of one ticket type now share a single line with their count. The line folds out to show the individual seats, and each seat can still be removed on its own. After a removal the list stays open, so removing several seats is quick. This works on the basket page and on the combined checkout page.
- **A calmer order summary on the combined checkout page.** The date and time are no longer repeated for every ticket in the list, which makes the summary less busy. The basket page still shows them.

### Fixed
- **The error message for a failed email is now shown properly on the website.** When an email could not be sent while a customer was on the site, the message could appear as a raw language key instead of text. The text is now available in the site language files, in English and Dutch.

### Check before you update
- **Overrides of the `cart_lines` layout.** The layout has a new option `showDate` (on by default) and now groups seats per ticket type. If you override `cart_lines`, compare your override with the new layout to get the grouped seat lines. Sites without an override need to do nothing.
