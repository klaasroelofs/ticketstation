## What's new in 2.19.0

- **Doors open:** a ticket now has an optional *Doors open* time (for example 19:30), entered next to the start and end date. The date is taken from the ticket.
- The time is shown on the events list, the event page and the seat plan page.
- It is a field of its own in the ticket layout editor, with its own position, size and colour, and is printed as *Doors open: 19:30* when filled in.
- It is passed on to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes and to the search engine data (schema.org `doorTime`).

## Check before you update

Existing tickets have no doors time, so nothing changes until you fill it in. Tickets with their own layout need the new field placed in the layout editor.
