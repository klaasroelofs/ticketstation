## What's new in 2.36.1

### Customer portal
- **A warning while the menu item is missing.** With the portal on and no menu item of the type *Ticketstation » My orders*, the Customer portal tab in the Configuration says so. When a menu item with the alias `my-orders` or `portal` exists, the warning names it, because it can take over the address of the page. The documentation now explains where the pages sit: behind the menu item of your shop (for example `/tickets/my-orders`, `/tickets/cart`), and in the root only when the shop is the home page.
- **Links only open when they are clicked.** The link in the portal mail and the link in the ticket mail are refused when another site tries to open them in an image, a frame or a script, so a page of another site can't put a visitor into someone else's orders. Clicking the link in a mail, in webmail or typing it in works as before.
- **Orders that the page no longer shows can't be fetched by their number either.** With *Show past orders* limited to a number of months, the invoice of an order older than that was still reachable for its owner with a direct address. That is closed.
- **The limits use the visitor's own address behind a load balancer.** With *Behind Load Balancer* switched on in the Global Configuration, the limits on requests for a link and on mailing tickets count per visitor instead of all visitors together.
- **Looking at My orders no longer makes files.** A missing ticket file is made when the customer downloads the tickets, not when the page is opened.

## Check before you update
- **If your site runs behind a reverse proxy or load balancer,** switch on *Behind Load Balancer* (System » Global Configuration » Server). Without it every visitor has the address of the proxy and shares one limit.
