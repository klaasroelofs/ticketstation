## What's new in 2.7.1

### Ticket layout
- **Fields near the bottom of a ticket stay on the ticket.** A field placed in the bottom 2 cm of a ticket, or below it, started a second page in the PDF, and every field printed after it moved to that page as well. This happened in the preview and on the tickets customers receive. A ticket is now always one page, and every field stays where you put it.
- **Fields beyond the edge are pointed out.** The layout editor now marks a field that lies (partly) outside the ticket in red, and the field list says *Beyond the edge of the ticket*. This helps to find a field you can't see on the ticket, for example after switching from portrait to landscape.

## Check before you update
- Open the *Ticket layout* tab of your tickets, especially landscape ones, and look for fields marked *Beyond the edge of the ticket*. Such a field is not printed; move it onto the ticket or switch it off.
