## What's new in 2.22.0

### Live updates for Google Wallet
- **Passes follow the ticket.** With *Live updates* switched on (Configuration, Wallet tab, under Google Wallet), the passes customers already saved in Google Wallet are kept in line with their tickets. A refunded or blocked ticket makes its pass inactive, and a new QR code from the Box Office is sent to the pass. Customers don't have to add anything again.
- **A changed event, when you say so.** After you change the time, the venue or the name, open *Mail the buyers* in the Events list and check *Bring the tickets in Google Wallet in line with the event*. Nothing goes to your customers' devices until you do that.
- **A message on the passes.** On the same screen you can also show your text as a message on the passes, with a notification on the device. The subject is the title. Uncheck *Send the mail* to reach the wallets only.
- **In portions.** A first portion is sent right away, the rest by the new Scheduled Task *Ticketstation wallet updates* (System, Scheduled Tasks, every 15 minutes). A pass that fails keeps its reason and is tried again. After sending, the screen tells how many passes were updated and what Google answered if something went wrong.
- **What you need.** The service account of your Google Wallet key must be a user of your issuer in the Google Pay and Wallet Console (Developer or Admin), and the Google Wallet API must be enabled in the Google Cloud project that owns the key. The Documentation explains this under Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.
- Apple Wallet passes are not updated yet.

### Mail the buyers
- *Send the mail* is a checkbox you can switch off, for when you only want to update or message the wallets.

### Documentation
- The Configuration reference now explains the *Reminder* settings on the Orders tab.

## Check before you update
- **Live updates are off after the update.** Nothing changes for your passes until you switch it on in the Configuration (Wallet tab).
