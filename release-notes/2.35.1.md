## What's new in 2.35.1

### Fixed
- **The background in the ticket layout editor fills the whole page.** An uploaded design was drawn smaller than the ticket in the editor, with a white strip on the right and at the bottom, while the preview and the real tickets were right. The editor now draws the design the same way as the ticket does, for A5 and A4.
- **The waiting list question can be answered.** When a customer chose more tickets than were left, the message asked whether they wanted the rest on the waiting list, but the button for it was nowhere to be found until they reloaded the page. The message now has the buttons itself: put the tickets that are left in the basket and the rest on the waiting list, or only the tickets that are left in the basket.
- **The last tickets turn the ticket into a waiting list ticket.** When a customer puts the last tickets of a ticket type in their basket, the ticket on the page changes at once: a red *Sold out* line replaces *Only a few tickets left*, and the button becomes *Put me on the waiting list*. A customer who clicks again on an out-of-date page gets a message with that button as well.
- **The texts around the waiting list no longer say the customer is already on it.** A customer is on the waiting list only after clicking the link in the email. The basket, the check and pay page and the page after leaving the details now say so.

### Changed
- **On a coupon, the ticket field is called *Valid for tickets*.** That tells it apart from *Valid for events*. It lists the parent tickets only, because a parent ticket always includes its child tickets.
- **The Coupons section of the Documentation page is up to date.** It described a coupon that could not be limited to events or tickets, and now explains *Valid for events* and *Valid for tickets*, how they combine, and that uses are counted per mode (live or test).

## Check before you update
- **A coupon that was limited to a child ticket keeps working.** That child ticket stays in the list of the coupon, so editing and saving the coupon does not drop it.
