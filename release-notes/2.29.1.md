## What's new in 2.29.1

### Improved
- **The payment provider is visible on every online payment.** The payment section of an order now shows the provider (Mollie or Stripe), with a *Live* or *Test* badge, and the provider's own payment ID. The transactions list has a new *Payment provider* column. A payment plugin from another developer shows up with the name it gives itself, as long as the plugin is switched on.
- **Provider details on the order.** Everything the payment provider sent with a payment is now on the order, collapsed behind *Show all values*. Only users who may see transactions get to see it, because for some payment methods it includes the name and account number of the payer.
- **The transactions screen is a payments overview.** Click the date of a payment to open its order. The separate transaction details screen is gone, and so are the *Open* and *Delete* buttons: a payment is a record that has to stay. Deleting an order still deletes its transaction. Dates now follow your timezone.
- **Shop mode switch.** It lines up with the right edge of the tiles, has room below it on narrow screens, shows the test mode message as a warning, and the message disappears after 5 seconds.

### Fixed
- **iDEAL is labelled the same everywhere.** Stripe payments said *iDEAL* where Mollie said *iDEAL | Wero*. Both now say *iDEAL | Wero*, on the order, the transactions, the invoice and in the plugin descriptions.
