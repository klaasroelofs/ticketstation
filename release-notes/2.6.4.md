## What's new in 2.6.4

### A reworked Box Office
- **Orders with tickets for several events** now show every event in the list, with the number of tickets per event. When you filter on an event, such an order still appears, the other events are greyed out, and the ticket count reads e.g. *2 / 5*.
- **Better search.** Search on the full name (e.g. *Jan Jansen*), the phone number, the start of an order number or the code of a ticket's QR code. A complete order number or QR code opens the order straight away, so you can scan a ticket at the desk.
- **New filter** *Tickets sent*, to find paid orders whose tickets were never mailed. The *Tickets not sent* item on the control panel now opens exactly those orders.
- A line above the list counts the matching orders, their tickets and how many were scanned.
- **Export** downloads the tickets of the orders in the list as a CSV file for a spreadsheet: one line per ticket. With an event filter on, this is a guest list.
- The list columns are clearer: one *Tickets* column shows whether the tickets were created, sent and downloaded, and *Scanned* shows how many tickets were scanned, e.g. *2/4*.
- The *Actions* menu is grouped into payment and tickets, with the new actions *Mark as paid* and *Send tickets*. *New reservation* is a green button on the left.

### Inside an order
- The toolbar has a *Payment* and a *Tickets* menu that only offer what applies to the order, including *Complete Order*, *Resend Payment* and *Mark as refunded*.
- The *Overview* also shows the events in the order, the payment method, the invoice (click its number to open it) and how many tickets were created, sent and scanned.
- The tickets are grouped per event, with the date and time of each scan and who scanned it.
- The screen reopens on the tab you used last, so after marking a ticket as scanned you stay on *Tickets in Order*.
- *Remove Ticket* is red and asks for confirmation.

### QR codes stay the same
- A ticket now keeps its QR code. Making the tickets again, for example after changing the Order Reference, removing a ticket from the order or completing an order that already has tickets, no longer makes the tickets the customer already has invalid.
- To replace a lost or stolen ticket, tick it in *Tickets in Order* and choose the new button **New QR code**. Then send the new ticket with *Resend*.

### Fixed
- Order prices and counts in the list could be doubled when an order had more than one payment transaction, and statuses such as *Blocked* or *Sent* could be taken from a random ticket of the order.
- *Resend Payment* no longer changes the order date. The automatic cleanup still gives the customer the full period to pay, counted from the moment the payment link was sent.
- Searching while on a later page could show an empty list: a new search or filter now starts at the first page. A search in the Box Office no longer filters other lists.
- The list no longer stops at the 1000 most recent orders.

## Check before you update
- **Create Tickets** no longer gives tickets a new QR code. If you used it to invalidate a lost or stolen ticket, use **New QR code** instead.
