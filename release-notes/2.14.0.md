## What's new in 2.14.0

### Mollie settings
- **Bypass mode has been removed.** To book tickets without an online payment, use *New reservation* (set *Already paid* to *Yes* for complimentary tickets). To try the website's order flow without Mollie, order a free ticket.

### New reservation
- **No more surprise logouts.** While a step of *New reservation* is open in the browser, you stay logged in, also when you spend longer on it than Joomla's session lifetime, for instance while typing customer details during a phone call.
- **Tickets stay reserved while you work.** The automatic cleanup of unfinished orders no longer removes the tickets or seats of a reservation you are still working on. The cleanup period now counts from the last time the reservation was open in the browser, not from the moment the first ticket was added.
- **Fixed: empty orders.** When the tickets of a reservation had been released anyway (the page was closed or the computer was asleep for too long), completing it created an order without tickets and, with *Already paid* on, emailed the customer a ticket email without tickets. Steps 3 and 4 now say the tickets were released and start the reservation again with the same event.

## Check before you update
- If bypass mode was on, your shop is open to all visitors again after the update.
