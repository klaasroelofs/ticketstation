## What's new in 2.6.9

### Shopping basket
- **One line per ticket type.** The basket no longer lists every ticket on its own line. Each ticket type gets one line with its quantity, the price per ticket and the line total. The customer changes the quantity with − and +, within the ticket's minimum and maximum per order, and the bin removes the whole line. The basket updates straight away, without reloading the page. Seats keep a line each, sorted by seat number.
- The payment page lists the order the same way: one line per ticket type with its quantity, and seats by seat number.
- **Reserved until.** The basket now tells the customer until what time their tickets stay reserved, based on *Remove Unfinished Orders after* in Configuration → Orders. The notice only appears while the automatic removal is switched on.

### Payment
- **Pay again after a failed payment.** When a payment fails, is cancelled or expires, the result page now offers a *Pay again* button. It starts a new Mollie payment for the same order, so the customer doesn't have to choose their tickets again. The button only appears once Mollie has reported that the previous attempt did not succeed, so an order can't be paid twice by accident.
- If an unpaid order has already been removed by the automatic cleanup, the customer now gets a clear message and is sent back to the event list.

### Seat selection
- **Legend on the seat chart.** A legend shows how available, taken and chosen seats look. Taken seats stay red and are now also striped, chosen seats stay orange and get a dark edge, so customers who can't tell red, orange and green apart can still see which seats are free.
- **New seat instruction.** The picture above the chart is replaced by two example rows in the colours of the chart itself: one that leaves a single seat over, and one right next to the sold seats.
- After an update, visitors now get the new look of the ticket pages straight away. Before, their browser could keep using the previous stylesheet until they did a hard refresh.

## Check before you update
- The basket, the seat chart and the payment result page have new markup. If your template overrides `cart/default.php`, `seatedevent/default.php` or `paymentresult/default.php` of com_ticketstation, compare it with the new version.
