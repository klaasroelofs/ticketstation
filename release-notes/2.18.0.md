## What's new in 2.18.0

### Your events in search engines
- **Event data for Google and other search engines.** The events list, the ticket page and the seat-chart page now carry machine-readable event data (schema.org JSON-LD), so search engines can show the date, venue and price of your events in the search results. It follows what you already filled in: the event, ticket and venue, the start and end time, the background image, the company name and website as organiser, and the price or price range of the tickets.
- **Availability and sale period.** An offer says whether it is sold out, only for sale at the box office (Online payments off) or available. When a ticket has *Use Auto Publish* or *Use Sale Stop* switched on with a date, those dates are given as the start and end of the sale.
- **It follows your display settings.** Without *Show venue* there is no venue in the data, the address and website follow *Show venue address* and *Show venue website*, and without *Prices in eventlist* the events list has no prices.
- **A switch to turn it off.** *Search engine data* in the Configuration (Display tab, in its own *Search engines* card) is on by default. Switch it off on a test or staging site that is reachable from the internet. That doesn't stop search engines from indexing the pages themselves, so also set *Robots* to *noindex, nofollow* in Joomla's Global Configuration on such a site.
- The Documentation has a new *Events in search engines* section under Events & tickets.

### Date and time format
- **Pick a notation from a list.** *Date format* and *Time format* in the Configuration are no longer text fields where you had to type something like `d-m-Y`. You choose from the most common notations, each shown with an example. A notation you typed yourself in an earlier version stays available as the current setting, so nothing changes until you pick another.

### Fixed
- The *Venues* button on the Dashboard showed a bed instead of a building.

## Check before you update
- **Search engine data is on after the update.** If you run a test or staging site that is open to the internet, switch *Search engine data* off in the Configuration (Display tab) and set *Robots* to *noindex, nofollow* in Joomla's Global Configuration.
