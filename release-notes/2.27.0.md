## What's new in 2.27.0

### New
- **Preview a mail template.** On the edit screen of a template, **Preview** shows the mail in a large window, with made-up details and exactly as typed in the form, so you can look at it before saving.
- **Send yourself a test mail.** **Send test mail to myself** sends the mail as typed in the form, with made-up details and without attachments, to the email address of your own Joomla account. The subject starts with "[Test]".
- **Restore the default text.** **Restore default text** puts the text a fresh installation starts with (in English) in the form. Nothing changes until you save.
- **Insert a placeholder with a click.** Click a placeholder in the list to insert it where the cursor is, in the subject or in the body.

### Improved
- **The Templates screen is easier to scan.** The mails are listed in a table with a clear name, a line about when each one is sent and its subject, instead of cards that showed the raw mail text.
- **The edit screen of a template is tidier.** The labels and fields line up, the editor uses the full width, and the description of `{walletbuttons}` says that the buttons only appear when a wallet is enabled.
