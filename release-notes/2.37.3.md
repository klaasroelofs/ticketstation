## What's new in 2.37.3

### Events
- **Moving an event now asks whether its tickets move along.** When you change the date of an event that has tickets, you are asked if the dates of its tickets should shift by the same number of days. Choose *Yes* and the date of every ticket of the event moves along, together with its automatic publish date and its sale stop (the time of day stays the same). The event's own dates for automatic publishing and unpublishing move along too, unless you changed those yourself in the same save. Choose *No* and the tickets stay as they are. The event itself is saved either way. Closing the question counts as *No*. A new event and an event without tickets are saved without the question.

### Box Office
- **A *Remove all seats* button in the seat selection.** On the seat plan step of a new booking, the button next to *Continue* removes every selected seat of the open seat plan in one go, after you confirm. Seats on another seat plan and tickets without a seat in the booking stay.
