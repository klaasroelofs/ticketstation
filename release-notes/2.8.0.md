## What's new in 2.8.0

### Permissions
- **Decide who may do what in Ticketstation.** Ticketstation now uses Joomla's permissions. Next to the usual rights (open the component, create, edit, publish, delete) there are separate permissions for the Box Office, making reservations, registering payments, finance (transactions and invoices), deleting orders and customers, and ticket scanning. The Configuration, the Mollie settings and the Templates need the *Configuration* permission.
- **A Permissions button** on the Dashboard and in the Configuration opens Joomla's permissions screen for Ticketstation.
- **Only what you may use.** The Dashboard only shows the screens a user may open, and buttons for actions they may not use are hidden. Someone who tries anyway gets a message that they are not permitted.
- **Registering payments is a separate permission.** Marking an order paid sends its tickets, so *Complete Order*, *Mark as paid*, *Mark as not paid* and *Mark as refunded* need the *Register payments* permission. Without it, a new reservation is saved as not yet paid, and the customer can pay through a payment request.
- The Documentation has a new *Permissions* topic with every permission, its defaults and an example group for box office staff.

## Check before you update
- **Managers get access to Ticketstation.** Until now only Administrators and Super Users could open Ticketstation. After the update, users in the *Manager* group can open it and work with events, tickets, venues, coupons, seat plans, the Box Office, reservations and ticket scanning. They cannot change the Configuration or Mollie settings, register payments, see transactions and invoices, or delete orders and customers. If Managers should not have access, set *Access Administration Interface* to *Denied* for them under Permissions.
- **Administrators keep everything they could do**, and can now change Ticketstation's permissions.
- These defaults are only set when Ticketstation has no permissions of its own yet, so the update never overwrites permissions you have set.
