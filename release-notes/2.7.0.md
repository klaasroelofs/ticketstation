## What's new in 2.7.0

### Ticket layout
- **Design your ticket by dragging.** The *Ticket layout* tab now shows the ticket itself, with your uploaded design (JPG or PDF) or the built-in layout as the background and every field on top of it. Drag a field into place, or click it to set its exact position, font size and colour. Fields can be switched on or off in the list next to the ticket, and the arrow keys move the selected field in small steps. A grid of 0.5, 1 or 5 mm helps to line fields up.
- **What you see is what gets printed.** The fields are placed the way the PDF prints them, in the ticket's own font, to within about half a millimetre. The QR code looks like the one on a real ticket, at the size you set. The *Preview* button still shows the exact PDF.
- **Warning for text that doesn't fit.** A field whose text runs past the right margin is marked in red. In the PDF, the part that doesn't fit continues at the left edge of the ticket, over other text.
- **Fields that are not always printed** are shown with a dashed outline and a short explanation: *Seat Number* (only on tickets with a seat plan), *Order Reference* (only when the order has one) and *Ticket Index* (only when an order has more than one ticket).
- **Starting from the default layout.** On a ticket without its own design and without any field positions, the editor shows the default fields. As soon as you change one of them, all default fields are filled in, so the others stay on the ticket.
- The position, size and colour fields you already knew are still there, folded away under *Advanced*. Changes in either place show up in the other. You still save your changes with the ticket's *Save* button.

### Tickets
- **You stay on the same tab after saving.** Saving a ticket used to take you back to the first tab of the edit screen. You now stay on the tab you were working on.
