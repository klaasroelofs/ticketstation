## What's new in 2.12.0

### Coupons for specific tickets
- **A coupon can be limited to certain tickets.** In the coupon form, *Valid for* lets you pick the tickets the coupon applies to. Leave it empty and the coupon applies to the whole order, as before. Choosing a parent ticket includes its child tickets.
- **The discount is taken over those tickets only:** a percentage of what they cost, or a fixed amount of at most what they cost. This is the same in the shopping basket, the payment, the Box Office, the VAT split and the invoice.
- The coupon list shows for how many tickets a coupon is valid.

### Captcha in the checkout
- **The checkout form now shows a captcha to guests**, the same one as on *Lost tickets*. It uses the default captcha from Joomla's Global Configuration and only appears when one is set there. Logged-in customers don't see it.

### Ticket layout editor
- **Alignment guides.** While you drag a field, a dashed line appears when it lines up with the left edge, centre, top or baseline of another field, or with the centre of the ticket, and the field snaps to it.
- **Undo and redo.** *Ctrl+Z* and *Ctrl+Y*, or the new buttons above the ticket, undo and redo your changes since the page was opened. This includes values typed under *Advanced* and *Load Values*.
- **Resize the QR code on the ticket.** Select the QR code and drag the square in its bottom-right corner to make it larger or smaller.

### Seat plans and the shopping basket
- **Clearer seat choice.** The seat page lists only the seats chosen on this seat plan. Seats chosen on another seat plan are shown separately, with a link to that plan.
- **Ticket names in the shopping basket link back** to their seat plan or ticket page.
- **Shorter instructions for single empty seats.** With *Prevent single empty seats* on, the seat page shows one short sentence above the examples. The details are under *More about this*.

### Fixed
- With search-engine-friendly URLs on, the ticket scanner opened from the *Ticket scanning* page showed the full site template around it. It opens without the template again.

## Check before you update
- If a default captcha is set in Joomla's Global Configuration, guests will see it in the checkout after this update. Place a test order as a guest to check that the captcha works on your site.
