## What's new in 2.5.4

### Payments
- New setting **Currency** in the Mollie settings: customers can now pay in euro, British pound, Swiss franc, US dollar, Danish, Swedish or Norwegian krone, Polish złoty, Czech koruna or Hungarian forint. Until now every payment was made in euros, whatever the configuration said. The default stays euro.
- iDEAL | Wero, Bancontact, KBC/CBC, Belfius and gift cards only work in euros. With another currency they are marked *Only in euros* and switched off when you save.
- *Website currency* in the Configuration is only the symbol shown next to prices on the site, tickets and invoices. When you choose another payment currency, change that symbol as well.

### Updating
- After installing or updating Ticketstation, Joomla now shows these release notes on the installer page.

## Check before you update
- **Currency:** after the update, payments stay in euros. If your site sells in another currency, choose it under **Mollie → Currency**, make sure that currency is enabled in your Mollie account, and check which payment methods remain.
