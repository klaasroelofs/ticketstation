## What's new in 2.37.2

### Seat plan
- **Pinch to zoom on a touch screen.** Zoom in and out of the seat plan with two fingers. One finger still scrolls the plan, and the zoom buttons keep working.
- **Click a chosen seat again to give it back.** This works in the shop and in the Box Office reservation. Removing a seat from the list of chosen seats still works too.

### Refunds
- **The refund texts now give the real timing.** With Mollie the tickets change after about 120 minutes, once the refund can no longer be cancelled; until then you can still cancel it in the Mollie Dashboard. With Stripe this is practically immediate. Before, the Box Office said "usually the next working day".

### Wallet
- **An invalid ticket in Apple Wallet now shows its message.** When a ticket was made invalid (for example after a refund), the notification on Apple devices showed the raw text `COM_TICKETSTATION_WALLET_INVALID` instead of "This ticket is no longer valid".
