## What's new in 2.31.0

### Selling
- **A ticket goes off sale at its end date and time at the latest.** Whatever the sales stop of a ticket says, or when it has none, the ticket is unpublished once its *End Date & Time* has passed, and automatic publishing no longer puts it back on sale after that. Until now a ticket without a sales stop stayed on sale after the event.

### Announcing a sale
- **Choose how the start of a sale is announced.** Events and tickets with automatic publishing have a new setting, *Announce the start of the sale*: with a countdown, with only the date and time the sale starts, or not at all. Events keep the countdown they always had; tickets are not announced unless you switch it on.
- **Announce a single ticket.** A ticket that is published later than its event is shown in the card of its event, with a countdown or its start date, until its sale starts. This is only for tickets of an event that is already published, and not for child tickets.
- **No more empty event cards.** An event without a ticket on sale shows *There are no tickets on sale for this event yet* instead of just its title.

### Search engines
- **The sale period in the search engine data** now ends at the sales stop or at the ticket's end date and time, whichever comes first, so it matches when the ticket really goes off sale.

### Fixed
- **The countdown no longer reloads the page endlessly.** When the sale could not start, for example because the automatic tasks were switched off, the page reloaded every few seconds. Announcements now only show while the automatic tasks are on, and a page that was just reloaded is not reloaded again.
- **The countdown is right for every visitor.** It counted down to the start time as read by the visitor's own clock and time zone. It now counts the seconds the website reports.

## Check before you update
- **Tickets that are past their end date are unpublished.** The first visit after the update unpublishes every published ticket whose *End Date & Time* has passed. If you sell tickets after the event has started, for example at the door, set a later end date and time on that ticket first. A ticket for which no end was filled in ends when the event starts.
- **This only happens while the automatic tasks are on.** The same switch as for automatic publishing and the sales stop: *Automatic removal of unfinished/pending tickets* in the Configuration, tab Orders. With it off, nothing is published, unpublished or announced automatically.
