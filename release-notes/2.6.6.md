## What's new in 2.6.6

### Choose the font of your tickets
- Each ticket now has a **Font** setting on the Ticket Layout tab, under *Size, Orientation & Font*. Choose from Raleway, Open Sans, Helvetica, Times or Courier; all text on that ticket's PDF is printed in it.
- Existing tickets keep Raleway, so nothing changes until you pick another font.
- The preview shows the chosen font right away, and *Load fields from other Ticket* now copies the font as well.
