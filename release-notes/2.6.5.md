## What's new in 2.6.5

### Every list keeps its own search and page
- Each list in the backend (Customers, Transactions, Tickets, Coupons, Events, Venues, Seat Plans, Waiting List, Invoices, Scanners and the Box Office) now remembers its own search, filters and page. A search in Customers no longer shows up in Transactions, and opening another list no longer lands you on a page that list doesn't have.
- A new search or filter always starts at the first page, so you no longer end up on an empty page.
- Customers and Transactions can be searched on the full name, e.g. *Jan Jansen*.

### Fixed
- The Seat Plans list could show only the seat plans of one event, without saying so, after you had filtered the Tickets list on that event. It now always shows every seat plan.
