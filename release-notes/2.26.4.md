## What's new in 2.26.4

### Improved
- **The ticket list in your mails is easier to read.** The `{orderlist}` placeholder now groups the tickets like the checkout does. Tickets without a seat are one line with their quantity, for example "2 x € 15,00 = € 30,00". A seated ticket is named once, with the booked seats listed below it. This applies to every mail that uses `{orderlist}`, including the waiting-list confirmation.
- **The Reservations screen shows your reservation grouped.** On every step you see the tickets chosen so far in the same way: one line per ticket type with its quantity, and seated tickets with their seats below, plus the total. Tickets of several events or ticket types are listed together.
- **Choosing seats for more than one ticket works better.** While you pick seats, the list next to the seat chart updates immediately. A free seat can still be switched to another price category after you clicked it, as on the website. Seats chosen on another seat chart are listed too, with a link to open that chart.
- **Every line of a reservation can be removed.** Tickets without a seat and seats on any chart now have a remove button, on every step. Removing the last ticket on the customer or confirmation step takes you back to the first step.
- **The first step remembers the event.** When you go back to the first step, the event you chose before is selected again. You can still pick another event.
