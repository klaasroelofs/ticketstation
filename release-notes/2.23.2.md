## What's new in 2.23.2

### Fixes
- **Live updates for Apple Wallet work.** In 2.23.0 an iPhone could not register a pass with the web service of your site, so no pass could be updated and no message reached an iPhone. That is fixed: passes added to Apple Wallet now register, and changes and messages reach them. Passes that were added before this update register by themselves the next time the iPhone asks; if one doesn't, the customer adds the ticket to Wallet once more.
- **A blocked or refunded ticket now shows on the pass.** The pass loses its QR code, expires, and says "This ticket is no longer valid" with a notification on the phone. Unblocking the ticket brings the pass back.
- **One notification per phone.** A customer with several passes of one order on one iPhone gets one notification for a message instead of one per pass. A pass shared with another phone still notifies that phone.
- **Messages sent to the wallets show on the Communication screen.** Under *What has been sent* you now see how many messages went to the wallets and when the last was sent.
- **Easier to find out what went wrong.** When a request of Apple Wallet to your site fails, the reason is written to the wallet log.
