## What's new in 2.6.0

### New seat plan editor
- The seat plan editor has been rebuilt. You now work directly on the chart: click a seat to select it, Shift-click to add more, drag a frame around seats, or double-click to select a whole row. No more typing database IDs.
- New tools place a whole **row** (with numbering counting up or down, by 1 or by 2, and an optional curve) or a **block** of rows in one click. Row names count up automatically (A, B, C…).
- Seats **snap to a grid** while you drag them; hold Alt to place them freely. The arrow keys move the selection.
- Change many seats at once: section, status (free or blocked), row name, size, **renumbering**, align and distribute, duplicate and delete.
- **Undo and redo**, and everything is saved in one go with the Save button (or Ctrl+S). Before saving, the editor warns about duplicate seat numbers, overlapping seats and seats outside the chart.
- Draw **shapes and text labels** on the chart, such as a stage, a bar or "Balcony". Customers see them, but they can't be booked.
- The seat plan settings (background image, seat size, colours) are now part of the editor, under **Display**. The separate settings screen is gone.
- The real-time view has been removed from the editor. To follow the sale and the entrance live, use the seat map on the scanning page.
- Sold seats, and seats in a customer's basket, can still be moved, but keep their section, row, number and status and can't be deleted.

### Seat plan templates per venue
- Save a seat plan as a **template** of its venue, and load it into the seat plan of any other ticket at that venue. Section seats are linked to the child ticket with the same name.
- **Export** a template as a file and **import** it on another site, in the seat plan editor or in the venue form.

### Seat charts on your site
- The seat chart on the site now scales with the screen, also on phones, and has zoom buttons. Customers no longer have to turn their phone.
- The seat chart in **New reservation** and on the scanning page scale in the same way.

### Invoices
- The invoice now shows how the order was paid (for example "Paid via iDEAL"). Orders without an online payment, such as box office sales, get no such line.

## Check before you update
- **Background images** of seat plans move to `images/ticketstation/seatplans` during the update. Open each seat plan once afterwards and check that the background and the seats still line up.
- The seat chart on your site now fills the available width. Check on a phone and on a desktop that it looks right with your site template.
