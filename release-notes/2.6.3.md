## What's new in 2.6.3

### A new control panel
- The most important information now comes first. Urgent problems (Mollie in test or bypass mode, a missing or wrong live API key, paid orders whose tickets were never sent) appear in a red **Action required** bar at the top of the page.
- New installations get a **Getting started** checklist: company details, Mollie, a venue, an event and a ticket. Each open step links to the screen where you complete it, and the card disappears once all steps are done.
- The key figures have icons, and *Tickets sold this week* shows the difference with the previous week with an up or down arrow. *On sale* now shows events and tickets as two separate numbers.
- New chart **Tickets sold per day** for the last 4 weeks. Hover over a bar to see the date, the number of tickets and their revenue.
- Availability and *Needs attention* are shown side by side.
- The screen buttons have a lighter, more modern look and line up neatly. A screen with open items gets a badge with their number, for example the waiting list or the configuration. Hover over the badge to see what it is about.
- Version, update notice, review and Ko-fi buttons and the license have moved to one compact footer.

### Fixed
- The control panel now follows the light or dark theme you chose in Joomla, instead of your operating system's setting. Before, a dark Windows theme could give dark headings in a light backend, and the other way round.
