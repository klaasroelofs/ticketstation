## What's new in 2.36.0

### Customer portal: My orders
- **Customers can look at their orders themselves.** A new page, *My orders*, shows the orders of a customer: the tickets with their events, seats and total, and buttons to download the tickets, add them to the calendar, download the invoice and add the tickets to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. It is off by default; switch it on in *Configuration*, on the new *Customer portal* tab, and add the page to a menu (*Ticketstation » My orders*). Do create that menu item: without one the page is reached at `/my-orders`, and a menu item of another page with that alias would take the address over.
- **No account needed.** The customer enters the email address they ordered with and gets a link by email. The link works for an hour and shows all orders placed with that address. The page gives the same answer for every address, so it can't be used to find out who has ordered, and the number of requests is limited.
- **A link in the ticket mail.** With the portal on, the mails with the tickets get a link to that one order. It works until the last event of the order is over, and the personal details on that page are masked.
- **Upcoming and past orders.** Upcoming events come first, soonest on top. Past orders are folded away and only offer the invoice, because the tickets of an event that is over can't be used any more. You choose how long past orders stay on the page: always (the default), or 6, 12, 24 or 36 months.
- **Refunds are shown.** A refunded order says so, and tickets that are no longer valid are marked and left out of the downloads.

### Send or download separate tickets
- **Pick the tickets you want.** Under *Send or download separate tickets* a customer chooses some of the tickets of an order and downloads them as one PDF, or mails them to themselves.
- **Mail tickets to someone else.** With a short message of at most 200 characters, the other person gets only those tickets as a PDF, with wallet buttons for just those tickets and no link to the order. The customer keeps the order, the QR codes stay the same, and whoever scans a ticket first gets in. The order history in the Box Office shows what was sent where.
- **You can switch this off.** The setting *Send tickets to someone else* on the Customer portal tab turns off mailing to other people; downloading and mailing to yourself stay.
- **Limits.** Each order can be mailed ten times an hour, and the page shows a message when the limit is reached.

### Joomla accounts
- **Link an address to an account.** With the option *On, with a link by email and Joomla accounts*, a customer who is logged in can link an address to their account: they ask for a link by email as usual, open it, and choose *Link this address to my account*. From then on they see those orders as soon as they are logged in, until they remove the link themselves or the account is deleted.
- **Never automatic.** An address is only linked after the customer has opened a link that was sent to it, not because it equals the address of the account. A logged-in staff member sees only the orders of addresses they linked themselves.

### Changed
- **The tab *Documents* in the Configuration is now *Invoicing*** (in Dutch *Facturatie*). It only held invoice settings; its documentation is updated and no setting has moved.
- **The hint in the ticket mail only promises what is there:** it mentions the wallet only when a wallet is switched on, and sending tickets on only when that is allowed.
- **Dutch: seats are called *stoel* everywhere.** In the cart, the seat instruction and the seat number label the Dutch interface used "plaats" for a seat in some places and "stoel" in others.

## Check before you update
- **Nothing changes until you switch the portal on.** It is off by default and, while it is off, there is no page and no extra link in the mails.
- **Two new mail templates** appear under *Templates*: *Link to My orders* and *Tickets shared by a customer*. With the portal on, the ticket mails (*sending Tickets after successful Payment* and *resending Tickets*) get a line with the link to the order at the bottom, or where you put the placeholder `{portallink}`.
- **The page has its own address, `/my-orders`,** and answers there even without a menu item. Create the menu item anyway: a menu item of another page with the alias `my-orders` (or `portal`, the name the page had in the release candidates) takes that address over, and then links in the mails would end up on the wrong page.
- **The update adds two small tables** for the requests for a link (only a hash of the address and the IP address, kept for a day) and for the addresses linked to accounts.
- **Mention it in your privacy statement** if you switch on the Joomla accounts: the addresses a customer links are stored with the account, and removed when the customer removes them or the account is deleted.
