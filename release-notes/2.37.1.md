## What's new in 2.37.1

### Box Office
- **Choose how many orders you see per page.**
- **The filters sit behind a *Filter Options* button.** The button opens by itself while a filter is on, and *Clear* resets them all.
- **New filters:** payment method, scan status, discount code, blocked tickets, confirmed, and order date from and until. Orders that the ticketcleaner removed follow the same filters.

### Customer portal
- **The link to My orders now opens in the Gmail app on a phone.** Since 2.36.1 the link showed "For your safety this link only works when you click it in your email" in some mail apps, because they open links without telling the browser that you clicked. Links are still refused when another site tries to load them in an image, a frame or a script.
