## What's new in 2.25.0

### Check before you update
- **Mollie is now a plugin, and the Mollie screen is now called Payments.** Updating moves your Mollie settings (API keys, test mode, payment methods, description, language) to the new *Ticketstation Payment - Mollie* plugin, and the currency and the choice to take online payments to the new **Payments** screen in the control panel. Nothing has to be entered again. If *Online payments* was off, it stays off. Open *Payments* after updating and check that Mollie is chosen under *Online payments* and that the plugin is switched on.
- **With more than one payment method, customers now choose on your website.** If you allow several methods (for example iDEAL and credit card), the customer picks one on the payment page before going to Mollie. With one method nothing changes.
- **Keep the Mollie plugin switched on while orders may still be refunded.** Payments, refunds and chargebacks of earlier orders are handled through it. Switching it off makes online payments stop, and the control panel warns you.

### New
- **Payment providers are plugins.** Other payment services can be added by installing their plugin, without changing Ticketstation. Developers can find a guide and an example plugin in the `developer` folder of the repository.
- **A Payments screen.** Choose the provider that takes online payments (or *Off*), set the currency, see all installed payment plugins with their version and status, open their settings, and switch them on or off.
- **The currency is set once, for the whole shop.** The list only shows the currencies the chosen provider can collect, and is wider than before, with currencies such as the Saudi riyal, the Turkish lira and the UAE dirham.
- **Currencies without decimals.** The Japanese yen and a few other currencies without decimals can be used: order totals, discounts, service fees, VAT and payments are rounded to whole units.
- **More payment methods at checkout for plugins that offer them.** The customer's choice is passed to the provider, so its own screen doesn't ask again.
- **Dates on screen follow the calendar of the site language.** With a language pack that uses the Persian (Jalali) calendar, the dates and times in the backend and on the website are shown in that calendar, as Joomla's own date picker does. Tickets, invoices, mails, wallet passes and data for other systems keep the Gregorian calendar. Sites with a Gregorian calendar see no change.

### Improvements
- **A cancelled or failed payment goes back to your website.** Before, a customer who cancelled could stay on Mollie's pages, because the website sent a list of methods even when only one was allowed.
- **The "checking your payment" page no longer waits forever.** After about a minute and a half it offers to pay again, with a note not to pay twice if the customer already paid.
- **Mollie's confirmation can no longer get lost when Mollie is briefly unreachable.** The website now tells Mollie to try again later, where it used to say everything was fine.
- **Refund and chargeback messages in the Box Office** speak of the payment provider instead of Mollie.
- **A payment in a different currency than the shop's is no longer accepted,** and an order can't be refunded by hand when the plugin of its payment provider is switched off.
- **An invalid API key gives a clear message** instead of an error page.
- **Updated documentation** with an Online payments topic for the Payments screen.
