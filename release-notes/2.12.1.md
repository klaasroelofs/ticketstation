## What's new in 2.12.1

### Google Pay
- **Google Pay is part of credit card.** Mollie has no separate Google Pay method: it shows Google Pay on its payment page within *Credit card*, on devices and browsers that support it, once Google Pay is also activated in your Mollie Dashboard. The Mollie settings now say so next to *Credit card* and in the description of the payment methods.
- **The Mollie settings check Google Pay.** When credit card is active in Mollie but Google Pay is not, *Credit card* shows *Google Pay not active in Mollie*.
