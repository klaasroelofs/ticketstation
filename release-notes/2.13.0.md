## What's new in 2.13.0

### Selling without online payments
- **Online payments can be switched off.** The Mollie settings start with a new *Online payments* switch. Set it to *No* when you have no Mollie account, or don't want to sell online for a while. The website then only sells free tickets. Paid tickets show *At the box office* instead of a quantity and an order button, and you sell them through *New reservation* in the backend.
- **With online payments off:**
  - The control panel shows no Mollie warnings.
  - The other Mollie settings are greyed out but kept, so switching back on needs no new setup.
  - The Box Office doesn't offer *Resend Payment*, and payment links sent earlier can't be paid. The Mollie settings warn you when orders are still waiting for payment.
  - Payments already started at Mollie, refunds and chargebacks are still processed.
- Existing sites keep paying online: the switch is set to *Yes* after the update.

### New reservation
- **Go on with the same event or ticket.** Under *Start a new reservation immediately* there are two new switches, both on by default:
  - *For the same event* starts the next reservation with the same event selected.
  - *For the same ticket* skips the choice of event and ticket and starts with the same ticket. If that ticket can no longer be booked, you choose a ticket as usual.
- **Joomla-style switches.** The options in the last step, *Already paid* included, are now switches like the ones in Joomla's own forms. Options that depend on the one above them are indented.

### Settings
- **"Send tickets directly" has moved** from the Mollie settings to *Configuration → Checkout & payment → Payment settings*. It also applies to free orders, and it stays available when online payments are off. Its value is kept.
- **Order numbers can grow past 99999.** After 99999 they go on with 6 digits (100000). *Next order number* now accepts up to 6 digits and refuses anything else when you save.

### Documentation
- The topic menu on the documentation page is narrower on wide screens, leaving more room for the text.

## Check before you update
- If you look for *Send tickets directly* in the Mollie settings: it is now under *Configuration → Checkout & payment*. Check that it is still set the way you want.
