## What's new in 2.6.1

### Seat plan editor
- Fixed: seats that were blocked in older versions of Ticketstation showed as sold in the new seat plan editor. They could not be freed or deleted, and a template or another seat plan could not be loaded over them. The update turns them back into blocked seats, and the editor only treats a seat as sold when it belongs to an order.

## Check before you update
- Seats that were marked as taken without an order become **blocked** seats. After the update, open your seat plans and free any of these seats you want to sell.
