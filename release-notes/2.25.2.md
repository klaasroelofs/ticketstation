## What's new in 2.25.2

### Check before you update
- **Customers now have one name field.** At checkout and in *New reservation* the customer enters a single full name instead of a first and a last name. When you update, the first and last name of every existing customer are joined into that one name. Lists and exports show it as one column, *Name*, and customers are sorted on it, so on the first name.
- **Check your mail templates and invoice address.** In a mail, `{name}` is now the full name, `{firstname}` is the first part of it and `{lastname}` the rest. The update changes `{firstname} {name}` in your templates into `{name}`, so a greeting does not show the first name twice; look at templates that use `{name}` in another way. The invoice address has a new token `%%FULLNAME%%`; `%%FIRSTNAME%%` and `%%LASTNAME%%` keep working.

### New
- **Fill in checkout details from the user's profile.** In *Configuration* > *Checkout* you can link each customer-detail field that is shown (salutation, address lines, postcode, city, country and phone number) to a custom field of your users (*Users* > *Fields*). A logged-in customer then finds those details already filled in at checkout. Details already saved with the order are never overwritten, and the button *Booking for someone else?* clears them.
- **A hint for a mistyped email domain.** Typing `name@gmial.com` now shows *Did you mean name@gmail.com?* with a button to apply it, for the most common email providers. The customer can ignore it.

### Improvements
- **Prices in the CSV export follow your price notation.** The amounts use the number of decimals and the decimal point of your *Configuration*, without a thousands separator or currency symbol, so a spreadsheet reads them as numbers.
- **No more repeating the email address at checkout.** The second email field is gone; it made the form longer and was mostly filled in by copy and paste. The address is still checked for a valid format, and the hint above catches the common typos.
