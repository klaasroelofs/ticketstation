## What's new in 2.16.0

### Tickets in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet
- **Customers can add their tickets to their phone's wallet.** The ticket mail and the payment result page show an *Add to Apple Wallet* and/or *Add to Google Wallet* button. One tap adds all tickets of the order; every ticket becomes a pass of its own with the same QR code as the PDF ticket, so the scanner accepts it as it is.
- A pass shows the event, date and time, venue, ticket, seat and which ticket of the order it is. Apple shows it on the lock screen around the start of the event. Passes can be shared from the wallet, for example with the friend a ticket was bought for; like a forwarded PDF, the scanner lets in whoever comes first.
- Both wallets are optional and switched off by default. Set them up on the new *Wallet* tab under *Configuration*: an Apple Developer account for Apple Wallet (no Mac needed, Ticketstation makes the certificate request itself) and a free Google Wallet issuer account for Google Wallet. The new *Apple & Google Wallet* topic in the *Documentation* takes you through the steps at Apple and Google.
- Choose a pass logo and the colours of the passes on the same tab.
- The buttons are added at the bottom of the ticket mail and the resent-tickets mail. To place them elsewhere, put the new placeholder `{walletbuttons}` in those templates.
- Passes are not updated after they have been added: a changed event or a new QR code needs the customer to add the tickets again with the same button.

### Control panel
- *Revenue this week* and *Revenue this month* now show below the amount how much service fee those orders paid on top, for example *excl. € 12,50 service fees*. When there are none, that part is left out.

### Fixed
- After an online payment or a free order on the website, the tickets were created but not emailed, even with *Send tickets directly* on. The invoice that is sent along with them (*Send Invoice After payment*) wasn't sent either. This happened since version 2.13.0. *Complete Order* and *Resend* in the Box Office and reservations were not affected.

## Check before you update
- **Tickets that were never sent.** Customers who paid on the website or placed a free order since you installed version 2.13.0 may not have received their tickets. On the dashboard, *Needs attention* shows *paid orders: tickets not sent* for upcoming events. Click it to see those orders in the Box Office, tick them and choose *Actions* » *Send tickets*. Where an invoice should have gone along, send it from the order with *Send Invoice*.
